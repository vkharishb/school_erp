# Subscription Module Policy — V1.1.DEV.19

There is no mandatory/Core ERP business-module bundle. The Platform Owner controls the module entitlement attached to each plan.

- BASIC: Dashboard; limited School Administration/Configuration; Student; Fee; Attendance (student attendance only); Reports limited to entitled source modules; applicable bulk upload. 200 students per school included; excess students are chargeable per student.
- STANDARD: BASIC plus Teacher Management, full Attendance, full Reports, Communication. 500 students per school included; excess students are chargeable per student.
- PREMIUM: all catalog modules. 999 students per school included; excess students are chargeable per student.
- CUSTOMIZED: Platform Owner configures Plan Details, Cost, and Module List. No automatic business modules are injected.
- PAYG: Premium module entitlement with configurable per-student monthly/yearly rate.
- TRIAL: retains the separately approved restricted trial policy.

Unentitled modules remain visible to admin-level school users as locked. Catalog modules that are not implemented remain locked even when a plan includes them.
